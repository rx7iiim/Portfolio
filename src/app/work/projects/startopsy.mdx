## Executive Summary

Startopsy is an AI-powered startup diagnostic and co-pilot platform built specifically for the Algerian and MENA startup ecosystem. The name fuses startup and autopsy — the idea being a full, honest dissection of a startup's health, risks, and potential before it fails rather than after. It provides early-stage founders with a structured AI-assisted framework to validate, plan, and execute their startup from a single platform — filling a gap that generic tools like Notion or ChatGPT cannot fill alone.

The MVP was built as a full-stack web application using Next.js 14 (App Router), TypeScript, Prisma ORM, PostgreSQL, NextAuth, and Tailwind CSS with shadcn/ui, with AI features powered by calls to an external LLM provider.

## Problem Statement

Algerian founders face a specific set of compounding challenges that tools designed for Silicon Valley startups do not address:

- **No structured methodology**: Most founders jump from idea to execution without validation, business model definition, or competitive analysis.
- **No Algeria-specific legal guidance**: Understanding SARL registration, NIF/NIS procedures, CNRC filing, and startup incentive programs requires navigating opaque official sources.
- **No AI co-pilot in context**: General-purpose chatbots lack the structure, memory, and domain framing to act as a startup advisor. They don't know your roadmap, your BMC, your competitors, or your financial plan.
- **Fragmented tooling**: A founder today uses Notion for docs, ChatGPT for ideas, Excel for financials, and Google for legal info — with no thread connecting them.

Startopsy addresses all four with a single integrated platform.

## Product Features 

The platform is organized around a sidebar-driven app shell with the following modules:

- **Startup Diagnostic**: The entry point. A structured form that collects the startup's core information. This becomes the "context nucleus" — every AI feature reads from it to stay grounded in the user's specific startup.
- **AI Co-founder Chat**: A persistent conversational AI interface that behaves as a startup co-founder, having access to the startup's diagnostic data and maintaining conversation history.
- **Business Model Canvas (BMC) Generator**: Automatically generates all nine BMC segments using the startup's diagnostic data as input.
- **Competitor Analysis**: Takes the startup's sector, value proposition, and market to identify and profile relevant competitors.
- **Financial Plan Generator**: Produces a structured financial projection grounded in the startup's declared business model and stage.
- **Investor Matching**: Produces a curated list of relevant investors (angel investors, VCs, accelerators) with match reasoning for each.
- **Legal-DZ Module**: A dedicated Algeria-specific legal guidance feature providing structured answers on Algerian startup legal structures, registration procedures, tax obligations, and government programs.
- **Market Validation**: AI-assisted market sizing, TAM/SAM/SOM estimation, and demand signal analysis.
- **Customer Validation**: A framework for structuring customer discovery.
- **Startup Roadmap**: A phased, AI-generated action plan that breaks the startup's journey into concrete milestones.
- **Pre-Mortem Analysis**: An anticipatory risk framework where the AI assumes the startup has failed 12 months from now and works backwards to identify the most likely causes.
- **Pitch Deck Builder**: Generates a structured pitch deck outline grounded in the startup's data.
- **Document Management & Dashboard**: Stores generated outputs as referenceable documents and provides an overview of the startup's current state.

## Technical Architecture

**Stack:**
- **Frontend**: Next.js 14
- **Backend & AI**: NestJS, FastAPI, LangChain
- **Architecture**: Microservices, CI/CD, Docker
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui
- **Auth**: NextAuth.js
- **ORM & Database**: Prisma, PostgreSQL

**Data Flow Pattern:**
Every feature follows the same pattern: a Server Action handles data access and AI calls server-side, a page component fetches initial data using the Server Action, and a client component handles interactive UI state. This keeps sensitive logic on the server while keeping the UI responsive.
