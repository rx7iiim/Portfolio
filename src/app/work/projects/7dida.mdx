## Overview

7dida is a fitness and nutrition iOS app built around one core idea: the week is the plan. Instead of handing users a rigid program they abandon by Wednesday, dida structures each day based on how many days per week you can actually train — then fills in workouts, meals, and coaching from there.

## Key Features

- **Adaptive weekly scheduling**: Tell the app how many days you can train and it builds the entire split around that — upper/lower, push/pull/legs, full body, whatever fits.
- **Workout tracking**: Each day's session is ready when you open the app — sets, reps, rest times, and exercise substitutions all laid out for your level.
- **Photo-based meal logging**: Snap a plate and 7dida estimates calories and macros. Daily intake updates against your target as you log.
- **AI coach**: A context-aware assistant that answers training, nutrition, and recovery questions tailored to your current week and history.
- **Streak and consistency tracking**: A dashboard that surfaces the metric that actually matters — showing up.
- **Onboarding-driven personalization**: The app asks honest questions during setup and builds a realistic plan from the answers.

## Technical Details

- **Platform**: iOS (Swift)
- **AI Integration**: LLM-powered coaching with user context (training history, current split, nutrition data)
- **Availability**: App Store and TestFlight beta
- **Website**: [7dida.app](https://www.7dida.app/)
